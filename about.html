The Professor

Branding Excellence in Accounting Standards

Dr. Debasis Pahi holds a Ph.D from the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. He is widely regarded as one of the most lucid and rigorous teachers of Ind-AS and IFRS in the world.

His distinctive strength is converting dense regulatory text into decision trees, journal-entry logic, real-world case studies and red-flag frameworks that professionals actually use — whether preparing financial statements, auditing them, or advising Boards.

He has mentored thousands of CA students, trained finance teams of listed companies, and advised on complex transactions involving revenue recognition, financial instruments, leases, business combinations and impairment.
